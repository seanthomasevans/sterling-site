Why Sterling

Anyone can quote Canada,
few can be trusted with it.

01

No roster, no agenda

We rep no directors. Your director and your idea meet no competing interest here. We are hired to execute your job, and only your job.

02

We stand by our bid

The bid comes line by line, and honest. We stand by the number we give you, and you never open a surprise invoice. When the scope genuinely changes, we tell you what it costs and agree it with you before we do the work.

03

Reachable, any hour

We are on your clock, not ours. Call at any hour of the shoot, for anything, and reach someone who can decide and act on the spot.
