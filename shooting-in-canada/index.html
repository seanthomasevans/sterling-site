Shooting in Canada

Everything the shoot needs, handled.

Locations, crews, permits, incentives, and the paperwork that gets your people across the border.

Locations
Toronto doubles for New York, Chicago, and generic America. Montreal reads as Europe. Vancouver covers the Pacific Northwest. One country, many looks, all within a domestic move.
Crews
Deep, American-style union crews in all three hubs. ACTRA, DGC, and IATSE run sets the way your team already works.
Permits and access
Local knowledge of what a location actually takes, and the lead time to lock it. Permits, clearances, and the authorities, handled so the shoot day is clean.
Immigration
Foreign commercial personnel enter as business visitors with no work permit. We supply the letters your crew needs to cross without friction.
Incentives
The federal and Ontario production tax credits are built for film and television and generally exclude advertising, so for commercial work the real advantages are crew cost, the exchange rate, and logistics, not the credits. We tell you plainly which levers apply to your job.
Currency
A structural discount on every Canadian line before any credit, so the same budget buys more day.

Have a job in mind? We will send references at your scale, a location read, and a ballpark before you commit to anything.
